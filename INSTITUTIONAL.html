TheSpark · Publication

TheSPARK is the Official Student-Community Publication of Camarines Sur Polytech Colleges, Nabua Camarines Sur. It is responsible with the release of published magazines and newsletters containg the events, accomplishments, and issues circulating the institution for the whole academic year. It also uses various social media platforms to disseminate news and other in and off campus happenings online. It upholds its mantra "Truth Knows No Limits".

Supreme Student Council

The CSPC-SSC encourages students to join the council to develop leadership, civic-mindedness, and a sense of responsibility. Their mission is to inculcate social consciousness, nationalism, and moral character among students while strengthening and protecting their democratic rights and welfare. By joining the council, students can represent the studentry, organize student activities, and participate in inter-student council activities. The CSPC-SSC also assists students with academic problems and other student activities.
