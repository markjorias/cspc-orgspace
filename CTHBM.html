Student Council

The highest governing organization in the College of Tourism, Hospitality, and Business Management. This organization represent the body of all bonafide students coming from different programs under the college namely: BS Tourism Management, BS Hospitality Management, BS Office Administration, BS Entrepreneurship, and BS Business Administration Major in Financial Management.

The CTHBM-College Student Council promote a spirit of cooperation among students, faculty, and staff; to coordinate and regulate student activities; to provide balanced programming in the areas of educational and cultural events, entertainment, recreation, and service to the school and community; to encourage student involvement and develop good citizens through experience, in a representative type government, and to serve as the official voice of the student body, and serve as an instrument through which the students may find channels to express their viewpoints to the College, do ordain and hereby establish College Student Council of College of Tourism, Hospitality, and Business Management.

Junior Financial Executives

The Junior Financial Executives - CSPC Chapter (JFINEX-CSPC Chapter) is a student organization affiliated with the Bachelor of Science in Business Administration Major in Financial Management (BSBA - FM) program under the College of Tourism, Hospitality, and Business Management at Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges. Established on August 25, 2023, this organization was founded with the primary objectives of promoting finance as a field of study, upholding excellence through financial literacy, raising social awareness, developing potential leaders, and nurturing future industry professionals through a wide range of activities.
