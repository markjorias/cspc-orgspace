College of Engineering and Architecture

Student Council

The College of Engineering and Architecture - College Student Council is dedicated in serving its constituents; the engineering and architecture students of Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges and the whole CEA college community.

The organization encourages students to be part of it by hosting orientation seminars and general assemblies at the beginning of the academic year to acquaint students about the student council's important role and objectives. It also promotes transparency through various channels of communication thus, setting a platform where students and the council officers can clearly communicate. These initiatives highlights the benefits of being part of the student council and thus, hopefully spark the inner leaders of the students under the college.

The organization provides a platform to cultivate your leadership potential. Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers

PSME-CSPC SU is an organization that develops mechanically inclined individuals into competent mechanical engineers. They focus on science, math, computing tools, creative thinking, environmental awareness, ethics, and integrating mechanical engineering with other fields. PSME equips graduates to plan, design, construct, manage, and maintain machinery equipment and conduct research in the field. Benefits of membership include professional development resources, networking opportunities, and career seminars.
